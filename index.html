Glowing Master

Glowing Master is a small automation tool that prepares short educational videos about science and everyday topics and uploads them to a single YouTube channel owned by the person who operates the tool.

It is a private, single-operator application. It is not a public service, has no user accounts, and does not collect information from visitors or viewers.

What it does

  • Researches a topic from openly licensed sources and writes a script.
  • Produces narration and visuals, and renders a video.
  • Uploads the video to the operator's own YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics.
